Safari Bookmark Checker — Apple silicon

1. Drag Safari Bookmark Checker.app into Applications and open it once.
2. In System Settings > Privacy & Security > Full Disk Access, add the app
   and enable it. Quit and reopen the app afterward. An older development
   installation may need its permission removed and granted again.
3. Enable Safari Bookmark Checker Extension in Safari > Settings > Extensions.
4. Click Read Safari Bookmarks in the companion app.

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Optional on-device AI reviews
require macOS 26, Apple Intelligence enabled, and a ready model.
The app reads bookmarks and never edits or deletes them.

Source, privacy details, and support:
https://github.com/KwisatzJim/safari-bookmark-checker-extension
